The problem

People decide using whatever is most visible: the salary, the distance, the brand. In the process they overlook factors that matter, lean on assumptions they never stated, and miss conflicts inside their own reasoning.

What BlindSpot does

It reads your actual reasoning and separates what you know from what you assume and what is still unknown. Then it surfaces blind spots, tensions between your priorities, and questions worth investigating.

What it never does

It never recommends an option, ranks your choices, predicts an outcome, or tells you what to choose. The decision stays entirely yours.

Process

How it works

Four steps that examine your reasoning, without choosing for you.

  1. 01

    Describe your decision

    Name the choice in front of you and the options you are weighing.

  2. 02

    Explain your reasoning

    Share what you know, what you value, and why you are currently leaning one way.

  3. 03

    BlindSpot examines it

    It looks for blind spots, possible assumptions, conflicts and missing information.

  4. 04

    Stress-test and refine

    Use the questions and the challenge pass, add what you learn, and run it again.
